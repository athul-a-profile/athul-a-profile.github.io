~ HOW THIS CAME TO BE ~

Bridging Scholarly Heritage & Modern Systems

Most digital spaces are built on accumulation. They want you to build a profile, grow an audience, post things that stick around. There's always something to maintain, something to check back on, some version of yourself you're curating for others.

Libraries are the opposite of that. A place where research is curated without commercial tracking, and scholarly work is preserved across decades for whoever seeks it next. Not a feed to scroll, not a network to perform for. Just a quiet room of human knowledge.

As a dedicated Library & Information Science specialist with honors from Pondicherry University and hands-on experience at IIM Bangalore, IIM Kozhikode, and IIT Gandhinagar, I engineer frictionless access layers for academia. Whether configuring Springshare digital platforms, developing custom library work management systems, automating workflows in Koha ILS, or setting up secure off-campus access, my goal is to make human knowledge open, resilient, and effortless to discover.
