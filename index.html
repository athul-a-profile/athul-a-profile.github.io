About Me

Digital Library Services & Research Support

I am a Library & Information Science professional, currently serving as a Junior Professional Assistant with the Digital Library Team at IIM Bangalore. My experience across IIM Bangalore, IIM Kozhikode, IIT Gandhinagar, and IIM Ahmedabad covers digital library operations, electronic resource discovery, library automation, and research support.

My work combines library services with hands-on implementation of Springshare platforms, SubjectPlus, Koha, and DSpace. At IIM Bangalore, I contribute to digital platforms, usage analytics, user education, and library web development, including a custom Work Management System. Previously, I led the Know Your Resources subject-guide initiative at IIM Kozhikode, developed its resource taxonomy and staff procedures, and received institutional appreciation.

I hold a Master's degree in Library & Information Science from Pondicherry University, earned with distinction, and am UGC NET qualified. I was also awarded an international bursary for the 2025 Digital Humanities @ Oxford Summer School, where I completed the AI in Research Libraries strand. My professional focus is on reliable library systems, effective access to scholarly resources, and informed use of technology in academic libraries.
