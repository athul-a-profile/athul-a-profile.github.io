Research Support Project

Digital Resource Guides

Created digital resource guides, tutorials, and web-based support materials for databases, citation tools, and scholarly resources.

Project Overview

This project focused on crafting structured online learning materials to help library users independently navigate complex electronic databases, search systems, and research tools.

Materials Developed

  • Step-by-step PDF & HTML guides for database access.
  • RemoteXs login troubleshooting instructions.
  • Zotero and Mendeley quick reference cheat sheets.
  • Journal finder navigation tips.

My Role

I structured content, designed user-friendly layouts, compiled quick reference steps, and tested resource access paths for accuracy.

Outcome

The resource guides improved self-service support for researchers, reduced basic reference desk tickets, and increased utilization of digital subscriptions.
