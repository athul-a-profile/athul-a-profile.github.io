Executive Summary & Purpose

The “Know Your Resources (KYR)” Subject Guide was developed for the Nalanda Library, Indian Institute of Management Kozhikode (IIMK) to help students, doctoral researchers, and faculty members seamlessly discover, navigate, and exploit the library's vast collection of subscribed electronic databases and research tools.

Rather than leaving patrons to navigate fragmented publishers and complex vendor search engines, KYR organizes institutional resources into curated, discipline-specific gateways. By systematically classifying subscribed resources by subject, industry sector, company intelligence, and socio-economic indicators, the platform accelerates literature reviews, saves researchers valuable time, and dramatically improves the return on the institute's digital subscription investments.

"The subject guide serves as a curated resource platform that improves the user experience, supports research and learning activities, and promotes effective utilization of the library’s electronic resources."

My Role & Responsibilities

  • System Customization: Adapted templates, responsive CSS, header branding, search widgets, and navigation pathways to align with Nalanda Library standards.
  • Architectural Design: Designed the multi-tier taxonomy dividing resources into Company Information, Socio-Economic Indicators, Industry Intelligence, and Academic Domains.

  • Visual Guides & Tutorials: Created Word-based step-by-step search guides with screenshots for subscribed databases and uploaded them into the live system.

10-Step Collaborative Methodology Adopted

To ensure comprehensive, verified coverage of 100% of IIM Kozhikode's electronic databases, a rigorous 10-step institutional workflow was designed and executed:

  1. Designing the Structure of the Subject Guide: Designed the overarching structural blueprint and identified target subject disciplines, industry categories, and corporate data areas.
  2. Identification of Topics & Keywords: Consulted concerned faculty members and researchers to identify academic research topics and keyword hierarchies reflecting actual curriculum needs.
  3. Allocation of Databases to Library Staff: Assigned each subscribed proprietary database to specific library staff members for thorough domain exploration.
  4. Exploration of Databases: Library staff methodically evaluated assigned databases to verify search capabilities, data formats, and relevance to target keywords.
  5. Preparation of Database Access Guides: Drafted detailed documentation in Word format with step-by-step procedural instructions and clear screenshots demonstrating search and retrieval.
  6. Comprehensive Coverage of All Subscribed Databases: Iteratively continued the documentation process until every subscribed database was systematically cataloged.
  7. Compilation & Quality Review: Compiled, cross-checked, and reviewed all collected documentation to ensure rigorous accuracy and formatting consistency.
  8. Installation of SubjectsPlus Platform: Set up and deployed SubjectsPlus Version 4.6 on institutional server infrastructure with database connectivity and user role controls.
  9. Creation of Subject Guides in the System: Built all primary guides, sub-tabs, and cross-discipline discovery pages according to the predefined taxonomic schema.
  10. Uploading Supporting Content & Live Launch: Uploaded step-by-step instructional guides, database descriptions, and visual walkthroughs to facilitate frictionless patron discovery.

Structure of the Subject Guides

The KYR platform is organized into four core guide collections designed for rapid access:

1. Company Information

Annual Reports Board Details Market Research IPO / Stock Market CSR / ESG Credit Ratings SWOT Analysis Financial Data CEO Compensation Mergers & Acquisitions Start-ups

2. Socio-Economic Indicators

Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) GDP, SDP and DDP Health Statistics Energy Indicators Household Amenities & Assets Infrastructure Data

3. Industry Information (17 Sectors)

Agriculture & Dairy Automobile Banking & Financial Services Chemicals Consumer Products Education Food & Beverages Healthcare Information Technology Media Pharmaceuticals Real Estate Telecommunication Textiles Transportation Travel & Tourism

4. Academic Subject Domains

Economics Marketing Management Finance, Accounting & Control Organizational Behavior & HR Humanities & Liberal Arts Decision Sciences & OM Information Systems Strategic Management

Software Architecture & Staff SOP

SubjectsPlus (Version 4.6) was chosen for its open-source agility, zero licensing costs, flexible database-linking architecture, and dedicated focus on library discovery services.

To guarantee long-term operational sustainability, I authored the official Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Creating and Managing Subject Guides using SubjectsPlus, establishing repeatable best practices for:

  • Guide Creation: Step-by-step walkthrough for initializing public guides with standardized metadata.
  • Tab Navigation & Multi-Column Layouts: Utilizing SubjectsPlus drag-and-drop grid interfaces to organize content blocks cleanly.
  • Editable Content Boxes: Structuring descriptions, direct database links, and vendor credentials.
  • Media Management: Standard guidelines for screenshot upload, sizing, and uniform border styling (border 5).
  • Box Reusability: Cross-linking shared database access panels (e.g., Bloomberg, CMIE Prowess, Scopus) across multiple academic tabs without redundant data entry.
